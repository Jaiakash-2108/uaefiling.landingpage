THE PROCESS

How to set up your UAE trading company in four steps

One team manages each stage. A clear document checklist and written scope are agreed before any work begins.

01

Book a 15-Minute Advisory Call

We understand your products, target export markets, ownership structure, visa requirements and banking needs. The right UAE trading license type and jurisdiction is recommended based on your specific trade flow, not a generic template.

02

Receive Your Recommended Setup Route and Document Checklist

We confirm the recommended free zone or mainland trading company route, estimated fees, applicable government charges and a precise document checklist. Written scope provided before you proceed. No surprises.

03

We Coordinate Your Company Formation, Bank File and Tax Registration

Your UAE trading company formation application is submitted, your corporate bank account file is prepared and introductions coordinated where suitable. Initial VAT and Corporate Tax registration requirements are addressed based on your circumstances and trade volume.

04

Receive Your Full Handover Pack

Company documents, next-step bank guidance, your compliance calendar covering trading license renewals, visa timelines, VAT filing deadlines and Corporate Tax obligations all in one organised pack ready for your operations team.
